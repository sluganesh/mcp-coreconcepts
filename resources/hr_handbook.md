# Example Corp Employee Handbook

*Sample content for demonstration purposes.*

## Working hours

- Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's agreement.

## Leave

| Type | Allowance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual leave | 24 days per year | Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year |
| Sick leave | 12 days per year | A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 3 days |
| Parental leave | 26 weeks | Fully paid for the first 16 weeks |

Request leave at least 2 weeks in advance, except for sick leave.

## Salaries and reviews

- Salaries are reviewed once a year, in April.
- Salary changes need approval from the employee's manager and from HR.
- Salary information is confidential and is shared only with the employee, their manager and HR.

## Expenses

- Submit expense claims within 30 days, with receipts.
- Claims over USD 500 need approval from a department head.

## New starters

- New employees complete a 90-day probation period.
- Each new starter is assigned an onboarding buddy from their department for their first month.
